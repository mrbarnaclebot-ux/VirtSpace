Transitive-dependency note
--------------------------
The task named two files (three.module.js + spark.module.js), but neither works
standalone — they are ES modules with hard imports that MUST also be bundled or
nothing loads:
  * three.module.js  imports  ./three.core.js            (modern three ships split)
  * spark.module.js  imports  three/addons/postprocessing/Pass.js  (FullScreenQuad)
  * Pass.js          imports  three
So three.core.js and Pass.js are bundled as required transitive deps. Their
bare/relative specifiers are resolved by the <script type="importmap"> in
viewer.html, all pointing back at the custom scheme (see viewer.html).
